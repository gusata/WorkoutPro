# WorkoutPro

## What This Is
WorkoutPro is a premium, offline-first fitness tracking app for serious athletes. It provides workout creation, live workout execution, progress analytics, body measurements, XP/achievements, local backup, and mobile-native timer/notification support without accounts or a backend.

## Core Value
A polished mobile-first training tracker that keeps all data local, works offline, and combines serious lifting workflows with gamified progress.

## Who It's For
Strength trainers and athletes who want private, offline workout tracking with session execution, progression analytics, and native mobile convenience.
